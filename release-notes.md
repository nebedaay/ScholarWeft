ScholarWeft 0.5.6 is a one-fix release.

### Citation popup

The `@` search popup now closes as soon as you finish a citation with `]` — `[[@smith2005]]`, `[[@smith2005|see @, p. 10]]`, or a plain `[@smith2005]`. Before, the `@` inside the finished citation was still treated as an active search, so the popup hung around until you typed a period. A period still closes it, as do Escape and moving the cursor away.

The only other change is that the [searching documentation](https://github.com/nebedaay/ScholarWeft/blob/main/docs/searching.md) now says so.
